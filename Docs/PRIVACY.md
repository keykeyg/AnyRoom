# Anyroom privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Anyroom does not collect, store or share any personal data with its developer. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

- **Sign-in.** You sign in to your Microsoft account (and later your PlayStation account) directly with Microsoft or Sony. The sign-in tokens they issue are kept in your device's keychain and are sent only to Microsoft's or Sony's own services.
- **Streaming.** Video, audio and controller input travel between your device and your console or the cloud service. Nothing passes through servers run by the developer.
- **Settings and logs.** App settings and diagnostic logs stay on your device. Signing out deletes your tokens.

Microsoft's and Sony's own privacy policies cover what their services collect.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/keykeyg/anyroom/issues.
